Predator-Prey Archetype Gridworld Environment (PPAGE)


A deterministic, modular, research-grade multi-agent predator–prey environment for studying coordination, pursuit–evasion, and emergent behavior in Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning. A controlled experimental laboratory with fully inspectable dynamics, pluggable perception and incentives, and reproducibility.

  • Became a top contributor to this open-source, PyPI-packaged multi-agent RL testbed (Apache-2.0)
  • Built PyTorch MARL baselines from scratch: DQN (Double/Dueling), Actor-Critic, A2C, A3C (async "Hogwild"), and a MixedTrainer for per-team algorithm asymmetry. Designed JAL-GT, a correlated Q-learning baseline solving a per-timestep correlated-equilibrium LP
  • Codebase (~81% test coverage) now being scoped for AAMAS/EAAI and JOSS submission

Implementing Hybrid Genetic Algos (HGAs) to solve the Colored-Travelling Salesman Problem (CTSP)


  • Built a complete five-component evolutionary pipeline from scratch—incorporating path-guided initialization, edge assembly crossover, mutation, and neighbourhood search—reproducing the Lin & Li (2025) framework for the Colored Traveling Salesman Problem (CTSP).
  • Applied the algorithm to a real dataset of Pakistani fuel stations (Shell, Total/PARCO, PSO) to resolve path duplication caused by rigid corporate boundaries, achieving an 11.5% distance reduction (10,114.1 km), even under strict single-brand constraints.
  • Modeled a "Collaborative Shared-Hub Logistics" strategy that treated competing stations within 15 km as shared delivery hubs, slashing total transit distance down to 9,137.7 km, and unlocking a 20.0% total optimization over the baseline.
